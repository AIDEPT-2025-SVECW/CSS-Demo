✍️ CSS Font & Text Styling – Indian Context

i. font-size

Controls how big or small the text appears.

Small text – Railway ticket instructions
Medium text – College notice board message
Large text – Festival banner headline

ii. font-weight

Controls how thick the text looks.

Normal weight – Regular grocery bill text
Bold weight – Important exam notice

iii. font-style

Controls whether text is normal or italic.

Normal style – News headline
Italic style – Quote by Mahatma Gandhi

iv. text-decoration

Adds decorative lines to text.

Underlined – Click here for Aadhaar services
₹1000 ₹799 – Old price crossed out

v. text-transform

Changes the letter case of text.

india independence day
INDIAN RAILWAYS INFORMATION
welcome to incredible india

vi. text-align

Controls horizontal alignment of text.

Left aligned – Paragraph in textbook
Center aligned – Republic Day banner
Right aligned – Amount column in electricity bill
