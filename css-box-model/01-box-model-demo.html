📦 What is the Box Model?

In CSS, every element is treated as a box. Just like a tiffin box has:

  • Content - The food (actual content like text/images)
  • Padding - Space inside the container around the food
  • Border - The tiffin box walls
  • Margin - Space between your tiffin and others in the bag
